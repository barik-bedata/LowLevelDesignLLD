### Question 1
A train running at the speed of 60 km/hr crosses a pole in 9 seconds. What is the length of the train?

- **A)** 120 metres
- **B)** 180 metres
- **C)** 324 metres
- **D)** 150 metres

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer:** 150 metres

*Explanation: Speed = 60 km/hr = 60 * (5/18) m/sec = 50/3 m/sec. Distance (length of train) = Speed * Time = (50/3) * 9 = 150 metres.*
</details>

### Question 2
The cost price of 20 articles is the same as the selling price of x articles. If the profit is 25%, then the value of x is:

- **A)** 15
- **B)** 16
- **C)** 18
- **D)** 25

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer:** 16

*Explanation: Let CP of 1 article = Re 1. CP of 20 articles = Rs 20. SP of x articles = Rs 20. Profit = 25%. So, SP = CP + 25% of CP = 1.25 * CP. Therefore, 1.25 * x = 20 => x = 20 / 1.25 = 16.*
</details>

### Question 3
A can do a work in 15 days and B in 20 days. If they work on it together for 4 days, then the fraction of the work that is left is:

- **A)** 1/4
- **B)** 1/10
- **C)** 7/15
- **D)** 8/15

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer:** 8/15

*Explanation: A's 1 day work = 1/15. B's 1 day work = 1/20. Together in 1 day = 1/15 + 1/20 = 7/60. Work done in 4 days = 4 * (7/60) = 7/15. Remaining work = 1 - 7/15 = 8/15.*
</details>

### Question 6
The difference between simple and compound interests compounded annually on a certain sum of money for 2 years at 4% per annum is Re. 1. The sum is:

- **A)** Rs. 625
- **B)** Rs. 630
- **C)** Rs. 640
- **D)** Rs. 650

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer:** Rs. 625

*Explanation: Formula for difference between CI and SI for 2 years is P(R/100)^2. Here, Difference = 1, R = 4. So, 1 = P * (4/100)^2 => 1 = P * (1/25)^2 => 1 = P * (1/625) => P = 625.*
</details>

### Question 7
In how many different ways can the letters of the word 'LEADING' be arranged in such a way that the vowels always come together?

- **A)** 360
- **B)** 480
- **C)** 720
- **D)** 5040

<details>
<summary>View Answer & Explanation</summary>

**Answer:** 720

*Explanation: The word 'LEADING' has 7 letters, including 3 vowels (E, A, I) and 4 consonants (L, D, N, G). Group the vowels together: (EAI). We now have 5 entities: (EAI), L, D, N, G. These 5 entities can be arranged in 5! = 120 ways. The 3 vowels within the group can be arranged in 3! = 6 ways. Total ways = 120 * 6 = 720.*
</details>
